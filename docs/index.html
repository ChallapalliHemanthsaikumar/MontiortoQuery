Example Queries

"What happened at 11am today?"
Found 5 observations: 4 heartbeat captures of the residential area, and 1 person standing outdoors wearing a yellow and gray t-shirt, holding a small object.
"Yellow shirt person details"
At 11:05 AM, a person wearing a yellow and gray t-shirt and dark shorts was detected standing outdoors in a residential area, holding a small object in their right hand.
"Show me all people detected"
3 people detected: person in yellow t-shirt (11:05 AM), person in green jacket near silver car (9:33 AM), person in dark hooded jacket walking past building (9:24 AM).
"Any animals spotted?"
VLM described a small bird perched on the ground near a gravel path in front of a building. Surrounded by greenery in a residential setting.
